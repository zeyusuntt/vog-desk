## What happened inside the desk on the held-out nights

- The review raised at least one flag on 82 of 102 nights (model versus memory-model disagreement 58, regime unseen 33, analog split 30, data gap 5). The DutyLead's gated adjustments were applied on 47 nights in the full desk and 41 in the no-review variant.
- Full desk: base probability (memory model) Brier 0.2199, after adjustments 0.2153. No-review: base 0.2199, after adjustments 0.2244. The same adjustment rule helped after a re-query and hurt without one; that difference is the review loop's measured value on this period: small and not established, −0.009 Brier with a 90% interval [−0.019, +0.001] at 7-day blocks; only the 14-day sensitivity excludes zero.
- Handovers: the full desk handed over no night (its re-queries resolved every forced case); the no-review variant handed over 8 nights, 5 of them events (rate 0.62 against the period's 0.38). Handed-over nights are scored at the probability shown and never counted as correct.
- Model calls: 102 nights, Lightning evidence summaries passed validation on every night (53 after one retry); the Ultra decision passed on 77 nights (53 after a retry) and fell back to the code's template on 23 nights, which issue the base with no adjustment. The traces record every failure reason.
- Label audit (docs/LABEL_AUDIT_heldout.md): the Department of Health's list has 39 Pāhala exceedance nights in our window, ours has 39, the same 39. Daily maxima agree within 1 ppb on 37; on 14 Aug and 25 Sep 2026 our preliminary AirNow maximum is 16 and 32 ppb above DOH's validated figure (the same hour, revised down in validation). One DOH row (27 Sep, 5 to 6 p.m.) is a daytime exceedance outside the window.
- How often the desk warns: at the frozen t* = 0.18 the full desk said "prepare for yellow or orange" on 87 of 102 held-out nights (37 hits, 50 false alarms, 2 misses). 40 of those were "prepare for orange" (33 from p ≥ 0.36, 7 more from the analog-maximum clause); 19 of the 40 were events. That is an alert on five nights in six, which is the alert-fatigue cost of a probabilistic warning in a season with an event every 2.6 nights; the relative value curve (figure above) shows for which cost-loss ratios acting on the warning pays against always acting or never acting, and the probability, not the warning, is what a user with a different cost-loss ratio should read.
- Effective sample size: 15 seven-day blocks with at least one event.

## Reading

The primary pre-registered claim holds: the full desk has a lower Brier score than the model it was given (−0.117, 90% CI [−0.157, −0.080]). The secondary claim added before the held-out run also holds: it beats the memory-free adaptive baseline (−0.031, [−0.048, −0.014]), and it beats a constant at the hindsight base rate by 0.021. The review loop's contribution is small and not established. Most of the gain comes from the memory model, the pre-registered pipeline refit on the verified nights the desk remembers; the frozen 2025 model is near climatology in this period (BSS +0.03) because the eruption's regime changed. At the frozen threshold t* = 0.18 the full desk warned on 87 of 102 nights (POD 0.95, FAR 0.58, CSI 0.42); a desk that warns this often is a desk for a high-exposure season, and the probability, not the warning, is what the school's designated person should read. The period has 39 events in 102 nights, far from the 2025 training year's 21 in 356; a quieter season would test calibration at the low end, which this period does not.

## Defect found after scoring

On 24 October, while building the results page, the desk's own G3 stream (recent event rate) turned out not to match forecast 7. The stream queried memory with a cap of 400 records ranked by relevance and took the newest 30 of those; once memory held more than 400 nights, that was not the last 30 nights. On the held-out nights the two differ on every night, by 0.16 on average (G3 Brier 0.273 against forecast 7's 0.246).

What it reached: the value of G3 shown to the duty lead before it chose an adjustment, and the prior the analog rate was shrunk toward. What it did not reach: the base probability, which comes from the memory model and reads up to 2,000 records, and every number in the tables above, where forecast 7 is computed from the labels. The held-out scores stand as the frozen desk produced them; they are not re-run. The live desk uses the corrected stream from 24 October (PREREG change 3), with a regression test.
